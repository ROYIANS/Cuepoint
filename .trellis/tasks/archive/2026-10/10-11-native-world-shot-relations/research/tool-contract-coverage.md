Ownership: the single new `tests/nativeRegisteredToolParity.test.ts`, with no product/spec/config changes. It selects each definition from the actual `BUILTIN_TOOLS` array, creates a real persisted thread/run, submits a fixture model response through `executeChatRun`, checks the stored frozen approval, uses `resolveAgentToolApproval`, and resumes through `resumeChatRun`. It does not replace the business commands or ledger with mocks. Both chat-completions and Responses run all six operations.

**34 focused cases passed.** For every entry, the tests prove no mutation before approval; real saved business effects; rejected approval preserves the full business snapshot; a provider failure after tool completion followed by run resumption does not repeat the saved effect; and a late ledger failure rolls the business rows, project changes and arrangement proposal back together. Additional cases prove archive restoration and linked-IP facts, mandatory archive/revert confirmation in every permission mode, stale IP/edit/revert previews preserving subsequent manual edits, and all five audio entries refusing a foreign project instead of retargeting.
