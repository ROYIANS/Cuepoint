## Limits and closure

The real API sample set is one MiMo take and one Flow work; no claim is made that all Suno variants or APIMart TTS were paid-live tested. Model exact-span checks were invalid for some nonempty business replies and failed closed as visibly unverified; original replies and committed receipts stay intact. Advice-only review returned valid empty claims, which does not prove perfect prose classification. Acoustic quality and full listening are not inferred from decode/playback. These are documented limits of the approved bounded design, not silently promoted to passing outcomes.

Music DAW/arrangement, continuation/covers/stems/MIDI, structured vocalist/section intent and heterogeneous film batches/semantic synchronization stay explicitly deferred. Deployment/Umami is operator work outside this release. There is no remaining development or acceptance step within this release scope.
